## Delayed-payment interest can now be switched on

Until now, interest rules could be set up under Masters, but nothing could
attach one to a booking, so no delay interest was ever charged. Now:

- **When booking**, the last step of the booking wizard asks for the delay
  interest: choose a rule, or "No delay interest". Nothing is preselected, and
  the booking can't be confirmed until one is chosen. (Staff who can't manage
  interest book as before; the wizard tells them Accounts sets it.)
- **Later**, the booking's installment schedule has a "Delay interest" panel:
  attach a rule, change it, or remove it, and "Accrue interest now".
- **Interest runs only from the day the rule is attached.** Attaching a rule to
  an older booking charges nothing for the time before that day.
- **Changing or removing a rule takes effect from that day.** Interest up to
  that day is charged under the old rule first; interest already charged is
  never recalculated.
- **Only simple-interest rules can be attached in this version.** A rule's
  compounding setting isn't applied yet, so attaching a compound rule is
  refused with a message.
- **A rule attached to an active booking keeps its rate.** Editing its rate type
  or rate is refused with a message (create a new rule and change the bookings
  to it), and it can't be deleted while a booking uses it. Renaming it, or
  marking it inactive, still works; an inactive rule stays on the bookings that
  have it but can't be attached to new ones.
- **A transfer** carries the rule to the new booking, with interest running from
  the transfer date.
- **New permission: "postsales.interest.manage"** (attach, change or remove a
  rule). Accounts and Company Admin get it. "Accrue interest now" works with
  either this permission or the existing waive permission.

## Before you upgrade

- **No rule is attached to any existing booking.** After the upgrade, attach
  rules booking by booking from each installment schedule; interest runs from the
  day you attach. The upgrade output says how many active bookings have no rule
  ("Delay interest: N active booking(s) have no interest rule").
- **The new permission reaches Accounts and Company Admin automatically only if
  you upgraded to v0.8.4 first.** Coming straight from v0.8.3 or older, the
  upgrade records each role's defaults instead and lists the permission under
  "System roles that differ from their default permissions"; grant it in
  Admin → Roles. (super_admin always gets it.)
- **Interest is charged as a plain ledger line, with no GST or TDS.** How delay
  interest should be taxed, its rate, grace days and compounding are questions
  for your chartered accountant; set your rules accordingly.

## Fixed

- Two interest runs for the same booking at the same moment (the daily job and
  the "accrue" action, for example) could charge the same period twice.
- The "accrue" action could charge interest on a cancelled booking.
- Deleting an interest rule silently switched off interest on every booking that
  used it; it is now refused.
